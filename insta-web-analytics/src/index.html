How to get your Instagram data

Instagram sends it by email, usually within a few hours.

  1. Instagram → your profile → Settings → Accounts Centre.
  2. Your information and permissions → Export your information.
  3. Create export, then choose your Instagram account.
  4. Export to device.
  5. Set date range to the complete timeline — not "past year". A shorter range gives you only the followers you gained inside it, while your following list stays complete — so the two can't be compared. Format JSON, media quality Low: photos and videos are never opened, so a smaller file works identically.
  6. Submit, then wait for the email.
  7. Drop the .zip below, exactly as it arrived.
