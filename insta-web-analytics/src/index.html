The ZIP Instagram sends when you request your information. Add your previous insta-history-*.json alongside it to see what changed since last time.

Nothing is uploaded Your export is read in this tab. The page's security policy sets connect-src 'none', so the browser blocks network requests outright.
Nothing is stored No cookies, no local storage, no database. Close the tab and every trace is gone — which is why you download a history file to keep trends.
Works with no internet Install it once and it runs offline, or use the single-file build that opens straight from your disk.
Media stays untouched Only the data manifests are read. Your photos and videos are skipped entirely — about 90% of the archive is never decompressed.

How to get your export

Instagram → Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Choose JSON or HTML, All time, and request everything. It arrives by email within a few hours to a few days. Both formats work here.
